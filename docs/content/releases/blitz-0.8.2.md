**A guide you can see.** Blitz 0.8.2 brings a new user guide that shows you every step. Setting up a provider, finding your way around Settings, building agents in Studio, connecting a database: each one is walked through with screenshots of the app itself, in light and dark to match how you work. And it's all inside Blitz too: **Help › User guide** opens it, offline, for the version you have.

### Get started in three steps

1. Update Blitz (it updates itself) or download it from the [GitHub release](https://github.com/retail-cortex/blitz/releases/tag/v0.8.2).
2. Open **Help › User guide**, or the [guide on the web](../products/desktop/_index.md).
3. Start with [Configure the desktop app](../products/desktop/configure.md): your provider first, then everything else Settings can do.

### What's new

**Set up any provider in minutes.** The new [Providers](../guide/providers.md) page covers all eight in one place: Gemini, Anthropic, OpenAI and compatible APIs, Ollama, Amazon Bedrock, Azure, Claude on Vertex AI and your company's gateway, with a key or a sign-in, and how to check it works. Every tutorial now starts there, so you're never stuck at step one.

**Settings, pane by pane.** [Configure the desktop app](../products/desktop/configure.md) explains every part of Settings with a picture of each: providers and keys, a workspace's own settings, appearance, your global agents, skills, jobs, MCP servers, connectors and secrets, permissions and the service.

**Studio, area by area.** [Studio](../products/desktop/studio/_index.md) has a page for each area, from the Overview that grades your workspace to agents, evals, skills, rules, guardrails, jobs, workflows and secrets, with the steps to build each one.

**Your data, step by step.** [Connectors](../products/desktop/studio/connectors.md) walks through connecting a database: add it, keep it read-only, test it, have Blitz write a query from a plain description, try it, and ask your agent.

**Pictures that match your theme.** Every screenshot comes in light and dark. On the web the guide follows your system or the site's theme switch; in the app it follows Blitz's.

### Good to know

- The screenshots show example workspaces and data, so names and numbers in yours will differ.
- Your settings, workspaces and conversations carry over from 0.8.1.
