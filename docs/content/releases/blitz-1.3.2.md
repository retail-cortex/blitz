Your organization's models stay on, week after week. Until now, a company policy that nobody had edited for seven days quietly ran out on your computer, and its models stopped mid-week, even though Blitz had checked in with your organization all along. Blitz 1.3.2 keeps the policy fresh, and smooths out the forms you use to set it up.

### Get started

1. Update Blitz. It updates itself, or run `sudo apt upgrade` or `brew upgrade --cask blitz`, or download it from the [GitHub release](https://github.com/retail-cortex/blitz/releases/tag/v1.3.2).
2. If your organization's models had stopped, open **Settings › Providers › Organization** and choose **Refresh**. They're back, and they stay back.
3. Open **Studio › Settings › Permission rules** to try the roomier rule form.

### What's better

- **Organization models that don't expire on you.** Blitz now renews your organization's policy before it runs out, whether or not anything in it changed. And if you're signed out, Blitz says so and where to sign in, rather than leaving you to guess why the models stopped.
- **Drop-downs you can spot.** Every drop-down has its arrow back, so a choice no longer looks like a box to type in.
- **Room to write a permission rule.** In Studio, the rule you're writing gets a full-width line of its own under Allow or Deny, instead of being squeezed beside it.
- **Every install counted, nothing more sent.** Blitz's once-a-day note that it was in use hadn't been reaching Retail Cortex since 0.8.11. It does now. It says exactly what it said before, and nothing about you: [Privacy](../guide/privacy.md) lists what it sends and how to switch it off.

### Good to know

- Blitz 1.3.2 renews the policy with any version of your organization's Blitz service. An updated service also renews it from its side, every couple of days.
