## 1. Purpose

A workspace's `.agents` folder holds what shapes its agents: agents, skills, workflows, evals, rules, guardrails, jobs. The desktop app shows each kind and lets every one be created, read, changed and deleted, without opening the files.

## 2. Requirements

- **STU-01** The menu bar is **Workspaces**, **Changes**, **Studio** and **Help** (`menuIds`; Studio with advanced settings, DSK-60a). It replaces the Agents and Workers menus (as jobs were called then). *Until v0.7.1* **Studio** was a button, not a menu, that opened the Studio drawer. *Since v0.7.2 (the owner's request: the only item in the bar that wasn't a drop-down)* it's a menu like the others: Studio's kinds, each with its icon (`studioKinds`, `studioIcons`), opening Studio on that kind, or, with Studio open, showing it there as a click on its icon would (`StudioDrawer`'s `ask`); the kind shown is marked (`on`, from `onKind`), and **Close Studio** at its foot closes it as ✕ does (asking first about unsaved changes; `Drawer`'s `closeRequest`). A job that failed since the workspace's jobs were last seen puts a red dot on the menu's name (DSK-80c) and "N jobs failed" under **Jobs**. The background runs are the status bar's (PAR-PAR-20).
- **STU-02** The Studio drawer (`StudioDrawer`) lists the kinds at its left, as Settings does its sections (*since v0.7.2* as icons only, each named on hover and to screen readers: the menu bar's Studio menu names them, and two lists of names side by side were one too many), in the order an agent is built, grouped with a line between each group (`studioGroups`; the Studio menu's separators are the same): **Overview**; **Agents** and **Evals** (the agent, and what proves it; spec_evals_042 EV-12) and **Skills**; **Rules** and **Guardrails** (what it follows); **Jobs** and **Workflows** (what runs, simple to complex; *until v0.7.2* Workflows came first); and **Databases** (*since v0.7.2*) and **Secrets** (what it reaches), and shows the chosen kind's list and editor. Each kind's pane fills the drawer's height and scrolls itself, and *since v0.7.2* its width too (Settings' 960 px measure no longer applies in Studio). The title bar has **Reload agent files** (DSK-91d), Studio's one refresh: it reads the agent files again and lists the shown kind again (a pane holding unsaved changes is left as it is). Each pane starts with its kind's name and folder (`In .agents/<kind>`), with its intro and group headings lined up under them. Closing the drawer, or another kind's pane, with a form holding unsaved changes asks first. **Agents** is the agent editor in its sliding layout (`AgentEditor` `slide`; DSK-91b): the workspace's agents fill the pane, and choosing one (or **+**) slides its form in over the list, taking the whole pane, with a back arrow, **Cancel** and **Save** (kept at the bottom as the form scrolls) that return to the list; back and Cancel ask first when there are unsaved edits, and a save with problems stays on the form. **Skills** is the workspace's own skills (`SkillsPane`; spec_skills_013 SK-100–SK-103): their list, each skill's form sliding in with General, References and Scripts tabs, and Add from… to copy or reference another. **Rules** is the workspace's rules (`RulesPane`; spec_gates_041 GT-08): the gates before and after the agent in the order they run, and the instruction rules. **Guardrails** is the built-in guardrails, switched on or off, and the workspace's own (`GuardrailsPane`; spec_gates_041 GT-28). **Evals** is the workspace's evals, their cases and runs (`EvalsPane`; spec_evals_042 EV-08). **Workflows** is the workspace's workflows on a canvas (`WorkflowsPane`; spec_workflows_043 WF-09); while a workflow's canvas is open the pane takes the drawer's whole width. Studio can be closed while a workflow runs: the run goes on, and the status bar and alerts follow it (WF-19); opening the workflow again shows the run. A request to show a workflow's run (the status bar, an alert) opens Studio on it. **Databases** is the databases the agents can query, this workspace's and every workspace's (`DatabasesPane`; spec_databases_046 DB-14). **Secrets** is the workspace's own secrets under the same kind of title (spec_secrets_039 SEC-08), each shown as `{secrets:workspace:key}` with its stored name. Every kind has its editor.
- **STU-03** **Jobs** (`WorkersPane`, spec_workers_023): the workspace's runs going now, each with **Stop**, then its jobs (state, schedule, description; a failed last run or an invalid file in red) fill the pane, with **New job** (the drawer's refresh lists them again). A job's view (DSK-91: what it does and may do (and the workflow it runs), its runs, and a run going now, followed again when the view is shown again, with a workflow's progress and **Open on the canvas**; **Run now**, **Enable as shown** or **Disable**, **Edit**, **Delete**, asked first), its form, or a new job's form slides in over the list (the shared `slide-pane` layout), with a back arrow; the form's **Cancel** and **Save** stay at the bottom. Back from the form returns to the job's view, from the view to the list; with unsaved changes it asks first. What used to open the jobs' drawer opens Studio on Jobs: the command palette, the status bar's failed runs (the first job that failed), a blitz://workers link. Seeing the jobs clears the failed-runs dot. The global jobs have the same pane in Settings › Jobs (WK-60).
- **STU-04** `Tabs` (`ui/controls.tsx`): tabs over one panel, the chosen one underlined, ← → Home End moving between them; for the editors that come.
- **STU-05** **Draft with AI** (`DraftButton`, **Draft**, or **Redraft** when there's text): a request (optional: empty improves what's there), then the model's draft with what changed, **Use** putting it in the form unsaved, **Back** asking again. `WorkspaceService.DraftManifest` (kind, name, description, the text now, the request, a script's language) has the suggestions model (else the reviewer's, else the main one) write it from the kind's brief (`agent`, `skill`, `script`, `rule`, `guardrail`, `eval`, `workflow_step`), secrets redacted from what it's sent; nothing is saved. An unknown kind or nothing to go on is `INVALID_ARGUMENT`; a model that fails is a `problem`, not an error. An agent's instructions have it.
- **STU-06** `{secrets:` completes as it's typed (spec_secrets_039 SEC-09) in the composer, an agent's instructions, an MCP server's environment and header values (global names only), the settings file and the Files editor: `global:` and `workspace:` first (`workspace:` only with a workspace), then the names in either scope; a name goes in scoped. Escape closes the list (not the drawer it's in). The text boxes (`SuggestTextarea`) warn of a named secret that isn't there.
- **STU-07** The names come from `ListSecrets`, kept for 30 s and forgotten when a secret is added or deleted (`secretNames`, `forgetSecretNames`); values are never fetched.
- **STU-08** The workspace's settings no longer hold its secrets: they're in Studio.
- **STU-09** *2026-10-18.* Where a form picks several agents or skills (an eval's agents and skills, a rule's, a guardrail's, a skill's "offered to"), it's a drop-down of check boxes (`MultiSelect`), not a wall of chips; with none chosen it says what that means (every agent, the active agent, none).
